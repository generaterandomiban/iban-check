# Code of conduct

iban-check is a small project, and we want anyone who uses it or contributes to it to feel welcome, whatever their background, identity, experience or language. This document describes how we expect people to behave in the project and what happens when someone doesn't.

## Scope

This code applies to every project space: issues, pull requests, discussions, code review, commit messages and any other channel the project uses. It also applies when someone represents the project elsewhere, for example when answering questions about iban-check in a public forum.

## What we expect

- Be respectful and patient, especially with newcomers and with people who write in a language that isn't their first.
- Discuss the work, not the person. Criticize code, ideas and decisions with reasons, and accept the same criticism of your own.
- Assume good intent, and ask before concluding that someone meant harm.
- Say so when you made a mistake, and fix it.
- Respect maintainers' time and the decisions they make for the project, including a decision not to accept a change.
- Keep other people's personal data private. This matters in a project about bank account numbers: never post a real IBAN, or any other personal or financial data, in an issue, pull request or test, and remove it if you notice one.

## What we don't accept

- Harassment, insults, threats, and offensive or demeaning comments, including about someone's gender, sexual orientation, disability, appearance, ethnicity, nationality, religion, age or level of experience.
- Sexual language or imagery, and unwelcome attention of any kind.
- Personal attacks, trolling, and deliberately derailing discussions.
- Publishing someone else's private information without their explicit permission.
- Repeatedly pushing an argument after a maintainer has closed it.
- Any other behavior that a reasonable person would consider inappropriate in a professional setting.

## Reporting

If someone's behavior makes you uncomfortable, or you see something that breaks this code, write to sec@generaterandomiban.com. You don't have to be sure that it is a violation before you report it.

Reports are read only by the maintainers and kept confidential. We won't share your identity without your permission. If a report concerns one of the maintainers, that person takes no part in handling it.

We'll reply to confirm that we received the report, look into it, and tell you what we decided.

## What happens next

The maintainers decide what action is appropriate, in proportion to what happened and to whether it has happened before. In increasing order of severity, the possible actions are:

1. A private conversation explaining what was wrong, with a request to change the behavior.
2. A formal warning, and possibly the removal or editing of the offending content.
3. A temporary ban from the project's spaces.
4. A permanent ban from the project's spaces.

Serious cases, such as threats or harassment, can lead straight to a ban without earlier steps.

If you think a decision was wrong, you can ask for it to be reviewed by writing to the same address.

## Changes to this code

This code may change as the project grows. Changes are made through pull requests like any other change to the repository.
